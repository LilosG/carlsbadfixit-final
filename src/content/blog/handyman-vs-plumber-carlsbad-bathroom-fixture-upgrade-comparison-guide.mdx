Most homeowners in Carlsbad face the same question when something goes wrong with their water: do I call a handyman or a plumber? The answer matters because calling the wrong person wastes time and money. Here's the practical breakdown.

## The Core Difference

A handyman fixes visible, accessible plumbing fixtures and components. A licensed plumber handles the infrastructure—pipes, water lines, sewer connections, and anything that requires permits or specialized licensing.

Think of it this way: if you can see the problem and it's not buried in a wall or underground, a handyman can likely handle it. If the issue is hidden, involves structural pipes, or requires a permit, you need a plumber.

## What a Handyman Can Do

A handyman in Carlsbad can replace or repair:

- **Faucets and aerators** — swapping out kitchen or bathroom faucets, cleaning aerators, tightening connections
- **Toilet repairs** — replacing fill valves, flapper tanks, wax rings, and seats; fixing running toilets
- **Drain cleaning** — clearing simple clogs with a plunger or snake for surface-level blockages
- **Fixture installation** — mounting new sinks, towel bars, or shower heads
- **Caulking and sealing** — waterproofing around tubs and sinks
- **Shutoff valve replacement** — replacing accessible shutoff valves under sinks

These are the jobs that don't require digging, cutting into walls, or pulling permits. [Plumbing fixture repair in Carlsbad](/services/plumbing-fixtures-repairs/) is one of the most common requests we handle, and it's straightforward work that saves homeowners money compared to calling a licensed plumber for simple swaps.

## What Requires a Licensed Plumber

A licensed plumber is necessary for:

- **Pipe leaks** — inside walls, under floors, or anywhere you can't see the full run
- **Water main or sewer line issues** — these are deep, structural problems
- **Gas line work** — always requires a licensed plumber or gas fitter
- **Water heater replacement** — most jurisdictions require a permit and licensed installation
- **Rough-in plumbing** — for new construction or major remodels
- **Permit work** — anything that requires inspection by the city
- **Backflow prevention systems** — specialized equipment requiring certification

If a problem requires digging, cutting into drywall, or getting the city involved, you need a plumber. Carlsbad has building codes and permit requirements that don't allow handymen to do this work—and for good reason.

## The Cost Difference

Handymen charge less because they're not carrying the licensing, bonding, and insurance overhead that plumbers do. A faucet swap or toilet repair from a handyman typically costs $150–$300, including the fixture. The same job from a plumber runs $300–$600 because of their licensing and liability.

For complex plumbing, though, the plumber's expertise often saves money. A plumber diagnoses pipe problems faster and fixes them right the first time. A handyman who misdiagnoses a hidden leak could make the problem worse. ## How to Tell Which You Need

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **Can I see the entire problem?** If yes, a handyman can likely handle it. If the issue is behind a wall or under the house, call a plumber.

2. **Does the city need to approve the work?** If yes, you need a licensed plumber. If it's a simple fixture swap, no permit required.

3. **Does it involve the main water line, sewer, or gas?** If yes, always call a plumber. If it's a faucet or toilet, a handyman works.

## Real Carlsbad Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Your kitchen faucet drips.**
Call a handyman. It's a fixture issue, visible, and doesn't need a permit. A handyman can replace it in under an hour.

**Scenario 2: You notice water staining on your ceiling and don't know where it's coming from.**
Call a plumber. The leak is hidden, likely in the walls, and requires diagnosis and possibly permit work.

**Scenario 3: Your toilet runs constantly.**
Call a handyman first. It's usually a fill valve or flapper—a $50 fix. If the handyman finds the problem is in the line below, they'll tell you to call a plumber.

**Scenario 4: You want to install a new bathroom vanity and faucet.**
Call a handyman for the fixture work. If plumbing lines need to be moved, you'll need a plumber for that part.

## When to Call a Plumber: The Winner for Complex Work

For anything involving hidden pipes, water mains, sewer lines, gas lines, or permit work, a plumber is the winner. They have the licensing, tools, and insurance to handle structural plumbing. Trying to save money by calling a handyman for this work will cost you more in the long run.

The key is knowing the difference. A handyman handles the visible, accessible work. A plumber handles the infrastructure. For most homeowners, that means calling a handyman for 80% of plumbing issues and a plumber for the remaining 20% that require expertise and permits.
