## How to Streamline Tenant Turnover with a Handyman

**Schedule in advance**: Don't wait until move-out day. Book your handyman 3–5 days before the tenant leaves so work is done while you're inspecting the unit.

**Create a turnover checklist**: List all known repairs (drywall patches, fixture replacements, paint touch-ups). A handyman can quote the whole job upfront.

**Bundle repairs into one visit**: Combining drywall repair, fixture replacement, and cabinet fixes into a single appointment saves time and minimizes labor costs.

**Take photos of the damage**: Send photos with your repair request so the handyman can prepare materials and give an accurate estimate.

**Confirm completion before the next tenant moves in**: A final walk-through ensures all work meets your standards and the unit is truly ready.
