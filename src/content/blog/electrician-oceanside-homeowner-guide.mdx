## Common Electrical Issues in Oceanside Homes

Oceanside's climate and older housing stock create predictable electrical challenges.

**Salt Air Corrosion** — Homes near the coast experience faster oxidation of electrical connections and outdoor fixtures. Outlets, breakers, and panel connections degrade faster here than inland. If you live within a few miles of the ocean, inspect outdoor electrical connections annually.

**Aging Wiring** — Many Oceanside homes were built in the 1970s and 1980s with wiring that's now 40+ years old. Knob-and-tube wiring or older Romex can fail, causing flickering lights, dead outlets, or fire risk. If your home is pre-1990 and you haven't had an electrical inspection, it's worth doing.

**Overloaded Circuits** — Older homes often have 100-amp service designed for 1970s appliances. Today's air conditioning, electric heat pumps, and multiple devices overload those circuits. We can diagnose if you need a service upgrade.

**Outdoor Outlet Failures** — Oceanside's moisture and salt spray kill outdoor outlets faster. GFCI outlets (the ones that trip when wet) need replacement every 5–10 years in coastal areas.

**Panel Corrosion** — Electrical panels corrode faster here. If your panel looks rusty or you're seeing discoloration, have it inspected before it becomes a safety issue.
